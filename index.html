About

I am a master’s student in the Parisian Master of Research in Computer Science (MPRI) at Institut Polytechnique de Paris. Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I worked with Prof. Qinxiang Cao on AI-assisted program verification.

My research interests are in formal methods and program verification, particularly separation logic and the verification of C programs. I am also interested in how AI can assist with large-scale, real-world program verification:)
